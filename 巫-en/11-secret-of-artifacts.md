If talismans are solidified spells, then dharma artifacts may be considered solidified talismans.

Materials first: some materials suit housing different spirit-qi, some are poor at housing it. To make an artifact, first choose a material that can house spirit-qi, then by the artifact's intended nature choose among materials.

Specialized, targeted things need some skill to make. But ordinary artifacts are beyond counting. Low-grade artifacts are either incense-offered or consecrated, or items left by the accomplished at death, or things long handled like polished jade and beads, fed with essence-qi-spirit. Generally, so long as the material is not wholly spell-dead, take it to a temple with strong incense, set it before the god-statue a few days, or perform a rite borrowing god-buddha power to consecrate it once through — and such low artifacts can be batch-made by the ton. Items long worn by the accomplished are steeped in the master's dharma-power. Ordinary people, lacking attainment and spirit-qi, must let time steep the item in their qi. Even so, good things can be raised. Anyone with the will: get a seal, carve the mountain character, smear cinnabar, handle it daily, raise it with care a few decades — then give it to me. Meet a connoisseur and it sells for hundreds of thousands, no problem. So know more things, and getting rich is not hard. Spells, talismans, healing, exorcism, artifacts — all can make money; but used to make money, they cannot be used to cultivate the Dao.

Higher-grade ones need special making. Best with some attainment — prenatal or above yin-spirit, better still; the ordinary, playing at it for decades, can get some effect too. Some artifact-making is much like talismans; divine-way artifacts likewise invite a deity's split-spirit to reside and work — only, no painting as with talismans; pure cunsi lodged on the material suffices. Divine-way artifacts, through the link between the split-spirit and the deity's proper self, can be replenished at any time; so unless the spirit-qi is worn away by accident, the artifact's effect is near-permanent, even permanent. Self-power artifacts are much the same, lodging one's own spirit-qi or split-spirit. There are also artifacts raised buried in yang-gathering, sha-gathering, yin-gathering grounds. Find suitable material, raise it in a yang ground some years: you can raise something with quite good evil-breaking effect. A fengshui man can bury it in a sha ground some months — also an artifact, with some exorcism effect; only, whoever wears it meets misfortune, bad for the body — better suited to harming people. This is not only raising an artifact but a cultivation of the sword-artifact school: after the body dies, the spirit merges with the sword. In which case cunsi on divine beasts, heavenly horses and the like might ascend one to serve as a mount for gods. In sum, all these drain spirit-qi and are troublesome. Best: you raise them, I use them — not as handy as the owner's use, but the treasure is the effort spared.

One who raises artifacts affects the artifact and is affected by it — divine way, self-power, alike. Sword-raisers are influenced by the sword, easily turned all edge; seal-raisers take on gravity; youtiao-raisers turn very greasy. These things, spoken plainly, are just so — nothing lofty. Meeting some future bluffer with spells and talismans, you will feel it only so-so.

One class of artifacts relies not wholly on spirit-qi effect but contains some formation material.

What then is a formation? A formation, plainly, is structure. Everything in the world is made of structure; a formation is the act, by composing or changing a specific structure, of achieving certain effects. A maze, simply put, is a formation — its structure produces the effect of trapping people with no way out. From simple planar patterns to complex three-dimensional matrices — structure. Spirit-qi formations have limited effect on reality, not the wondrous workings claimed. Perhaps cultivation high enough to affect material form, down to the finest detail, would be a formidable power. After all, even a nuclear bomb is a matter of a few atomic structures. But one high enough for that needs no formations or artifacts at all.
