On the matter of seeding we have often spoken — that seeds are no good, that seeds are the divine way, that cultivating by seed is sewing for another's wedding, under another's control. But we never gave it systematic treatment; today we speak of it in detail.

Cultivation of the seeding type means leaning on gods, ancestors, immortals, and Buddhas, using one's own spirit-qi or cultivation to grow the seed they planted, until finally one is made a god, ancestor, immortal, or Buddha avatar with some self-awareness.

If self-power cultivation is planting your own tree and picking your own fruit, then seed cultivation is someone giving you a seed: you plant, he picks. Of course the one who gives the seed looks after you a little, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□.

Seeds divide into two kinds. One is the ghost-god seed, planted by spirit-bodies and ghost-things, incense-fire gods, incense-fire Buddhas. The other is the ancestor seed, planted by an ancestor who attained true immortality or true Buddhahood by self-power. What people call ancestor seeds actually varies greatly — ancestors need not truly have great attainment. Some ancestors have accomplished true immortality and Buddhahood; some may be only yin-spirits and low ghosts; some may have become incense-fire gods or servants of gods; some long since scattered, mere gods conjured by worship. And ancestors vary in good and bad: some simply delight in raising up juniors; others — purposes not so certain.

Seeds spread by many routes: some passed to the next generation through seeding, empowerment, passing the method, deity-transmission, yin-transmission and the like; some through certain religious rites or other religious activities; some in the course of practice or spell-training, where a divine-way step is involved — visualizing deities, chanting spells, calling names, evoking gods and Buddhas to gain a seed; some from long handling or wearing dharma objects, forming a link with gods and Buddhas; some from sincere offering and worship, chanting sutras and spells, becoming a devout adherent, evoking gods and Buddhas by faith-force. And some: a person goes to a religious site or near a god-statue □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□, and gets a random shot — one lucky winner handed a seed in passing, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□. Put it this way: even a glance at a photograph carries a tiny chance of being seeded. Then there is contact with religious personnel, possessed chuma mediums and the like — human-to-human transmission.

Talismans, spells, cunsi on deities, visualizing the master, reading sutras, chanting holy names, worshipping gods, invoking dharma-protectors — all can get you seeded or possessed. □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□; stained deeply, the road ahead in cultivation is gone; what you cultivate no longer belongs to you. Think: you toil at cultivation as at carrying a child — forming the golden elixir, raising the yang-spirit — and after all that birth, you find the child is not yours, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□, another man's. Laughable, is it not?

The gap between seeds is great — greater than the gap between rich and poor. Some seeds, like those given in transmission to cultivators, return well, so both quality and quantity are large. What an ordinary adherent evokes, or gets at random □□□□□□□, is poorer and smaller — at any rate the giver does not lose. But quality and quantity both change with circumstance: if your aptitude is high, your ability strong, the project excellent, showing high investment value, the master adds investment. If you lose money continuously, bringing him little return — well, half-hearted; at least after death your soul is his.

Among these kinds, only ancestor seeds are not necessarily malicious; other seeds certainly exact a price. So □□□□ can be cultivated; □□□ do not take up in faith.

What is a seed? A seed is in fact a bit of the spirit-qi of a god, Buddha, or ancestor — one might say a bit of split-spirit. □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□. Because this spirit-qi is higher in quality than your own, the seed becomes your spirit-qi core; your cultivation feeds the seed with your own or drawn spirit-qi, growing this core. This is the process of turning your spirit-qi into higher-quality spirit-qi — or, the process of higher-quality spirit-qi assimilating you. Hence the seed's quality is roughly your cultivation's ceiling: the seeded can never surpass the seeder. The seeded one's cultivation can benefit the seeder, but mostly only swells dharma-power; it cannot raise the seeder past his own level.

That the seeded cannot surpass a deity-seed's seeder rests on the divine spirit's incense-fire nature — it cannot transcend. Ancestor seeds □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□, and for you to surpass him and become the principal, swallowing him instead — think, and it is hardly likely. So surpassing is not allowed: either you are collected early as you near his realm, or the link is cut and you are let go alive. So different seeds yield different fruits; by circumstance, the seeded may at most attain differently.

Among deities: some are orthodox gods of quasi-yang level; some are wild gods, yin soldiers, dharma-protectors, chuma sorts. Immortals and Buddhas accomplished through incense-fire, though named immortals and Buddhas, by category are still deities. With such a seed, success means serving as yin soldier or dharma-protector, even as angel or heavenly officer, at most a servant-god serving a high deity, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□. Your spirit-qi — your dharma-body — is owned by the deity, though you still have use of it, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□. You do have a degree of autonomy; but because your thinking is eroded, you will believe your autonomy complete, unaware that your thought is influenced and controlled.

Truly accomplished immortals and Buddhas are another kind: they have true great attainment in their own spirit-qi, and can remain untouched by incense-fire thought. By their own choice they may wholly isolate and destroy the incense-fire, or ignore it and let it gather naturally into a god, or use it as a god-split-spirit to handle affairs for them, even make a dharma object of it. If what you get is the seed of such an isolated god or god-split-spirit, the case is as before. If it is the seed of a true immortal or Buddha, then you too may attain highly — although □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□, for to him you are not much use — at most kept as a fallback, a spare tire.

And then yin-spirits, low ghosts, demons, spirit-things: some possess directly; some do the whole deity-transmission or yin-transmission, even run an agent to pass the method. Their seeds are dangerous: the seeded one's soul is basically assimilated in life and eaten after death.

In fact, split-spirits arriving by seeding, empowerment, god-man resonance, or artifact transmission are muddled at first; split-spirits differ in intelligence, and one cannot split off so many conscious ones. Purely because this bit of qi is higher than the recipient's, it automatically becomes the core of his essence-qi-spirit cultivation; as the bit grows, the link with the god or Buddha proper tightens, awareness strengthens, and influence on the host's thinking grows. If the recipient has clear understanding, it can be refined away in the early stage. If he wants both self-power cultivation and borrowed dharma for spells — that is walking a wire, for constraining high-grade qi with low-grade qi is extremely hard.

So seeds have their good side: seeded, carried by gods, Buddhas, and ancestors, cultivation is easy. Without deep attainment one can command spells and powers — handy for earning a living. But every gain has its cost.

A thing given free with so many benefits — does the sky truly rain pies? The buyer's shrewdness is no match for the seller's; gods and Buddhas do no losing business. The more given free, the more they get. Take his silver, and you sell him your life. Any wit at all and you understand: the free is the dearest.

Some say: I will use the seed to cultivate, then visualize a sword of wisdom and cut it off. Once the seed is grown, no visualized sword will cut it; by then your spirit and the spirit's spirit are hard to tell apart. Even if not fully controlled, escape takes unshakable conviction. Visualization done well works somewhat against foreign possession; against seeds and long-dwelling things it is not enough.

Besides, receiving his seed, once accomplished you are his avatar; your self-awareness is deeply affected — you will not think of any sword of wisdom at all.

Ordinary people believing in gods and Buddhas — fine, a spiritual anchor, nothing too bad. But cultivators worshipping them are the gods' and Buddhas' first targets for seeding and control. Once a god-Buddha seed holds more than half of a cultivator, the man, though still visible, is in fact dead — only lingering; beyond saving. Generally, one of these symptoms □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□ appearing means at least forty percent. Past that stage, the essence is already a god-buddha avatar; the remaining limited self-awareness is a skin, a bit on the water above the iceberg. Independent awareness, though, is only a small part of one's spirit and soul; so even a god-buddha split-spirit can have semi-independent awareness.

To be plainer: one seeded □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□ first has Youjing, one of the three hun, assimilated, and Shuangling polluted and controlled. With one of three hun gone and one controlled, what self is left to speak of? In the end even Taiguang is polluted or assimilated. □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□. Some keep part of Shuangling, but the Youjing assimilated by the god-buddha naturally cannot abide self-power people, and manipulates your Shuangling to launch verbal or mental attacks. Even more: his thinking warped by seed or possession, he will subconsciously skip over your words against god-buddha control, losing logic on this front — even blanking briefly on seeing them. Many religious people and chuma-possessed I have met are like this. Such is the fruit of one's thinking warped or shielded by an external spirit.

On matters not touching this, he stays quite clear, unaffected. Many who believe in this religion or that gradually seem replaced by another person — in fact part of their soul has been assimilated.

A hard-cultivating man unknowingly replaced and handled like a puppet, unaware — a pitiable thing. This is the sorrow of being seeded: the heart warped step by step, in truth the same as the Cthulhu Mythos — only the Mythos exaggerates the speed. Even the mind-nature everyone thinks they have cultivated is often just thinking influenced by possession or gods and Buddhas, gradually suppressing one's own autonomous thought. But they do not know it; bystanders see more clearly.

Many hold self-nature and consciousness to be one: keep self-awareness and the self is not lost.

Not so. A devout adherent, say: beyond the shallow layer of consciousness still partly autonomous, the rest is assimilated by gods and Buddhas. There is a puppet-play where the hand goes inside the puppet — just so do gods and Buddhas handle adherents; from the seats it looks lively, when it is only the craftsman's hand wearing a costume. It is as if your computer's top privileges were seized by a virus, leaving you for now a desktop still looking the same.

The Hu religion's dharma says you do not exist at all: you are a consciousness arisen from chance causes when you were born into this world, roughly a piece of artificial intelligence; when you die you vanish, and your self-nature goes to reincarnation.

The ordinary adherent not deeply in keeps self-nature; the devout adherent has no self-nature left, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□, so when they die it is a homecoming. It is as if you too are unborn and undying — you are he, no mistake, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□. □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□; no matter.

From the Daoist temper of freedom, accepting seeding and empowerment is nothing other than □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□ — life and fate in another's hand; one thought reclaims your dharma-power, like the Wedding-Garment Art; a lifetime's toil gone to nothing, □□□□□□□□□□□□□□□□.

Only ancestor seeds leave that one way out — and it must be an ancestor of real attainment, and even then one hopes only for his kindness: perhaps when you have attained, the link can be directly isolated. But whatever his purpose, good or ill, this assimilation of qi is a principle of qi, a natural phenomenon; the process is not his to decide.

In truth, holding firmly to one's road — a road that makes sense in theory — is fine. Riding along is no wrong either; the ride reaches the destination quicker. It comes down to personal choice: on a clear understanding, choose for yourself; only not to be cheated. Choose yourself, bear the consequence yourself, no regret. I hold up self-power — the Daoist temper of freedom. I preach self-power — the requirement of my own line for its disciples. I do not exclude others' other choices; it is they who treat self-power as a mortal foe. I do not oppose others' religion either; but some believers are ever aggressive and exclusionary. So: do not speak to me; well water and river water keep apart — each playing his own game is best.
